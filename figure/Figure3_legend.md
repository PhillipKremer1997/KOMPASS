# Figure 3 — legend (draft)

**Figure 3. Treatment course, glucocorticoid exposure and serological
response in refractory pulmonary granulomatosis with polyangiitis treated
with teclistamab.**

All panels share a common time axis given in days from the first
teclistamab application; the shaded band marks the teclistamab treatment
window (days 0–25).

**(A)** Treatment regimen since first diagnosis. Blocks denote single
administrations, the bar denotes continuous treatment: seven sessions of
plasma exchange; cyclophosphamide 900 mg and 1 g at induction and 1.4 g ×
3 at re-induction; rituximab 1 g × 2, 500 mg and 500 mg; avacopan 30 mg
twice daily; daratumumab 1800 mg subcutaneously × 4; teclistamab
subcutaneously according to the step-up schedule (0.06, 0.3 and 4 × 1.5
mg/kg). Inverted triangles indicate the chest CT examinations shown in
Figure 1A–C.

**(B)** Anti-proteinase 3 (anti-PR3) IgG autoantibodies (U/mL, determined
by immunosorbent assay, normal < 2 U/mL). Note the axis break. Open
symbols denote values below the limit of quantification, plotted at the
assay floor. Anti-PR3 IgG normalized during daratumumab treatment,
whereas destructive endobronchial disease persisted (Figures 2 and 4).

**(C)** CD19+ B cells in peripheral blood (cells/µL). Repopulation to
22 cells/µL on day −128 preceded the serological relapse shown in (B);
B-cell depletion was sustained throughout teclistamab treatment and
follow-up.

**(D)** Glucocorticoid exposure. The step curve shows the daily oral
prednisolone dose, carried forward between documented dose changes;
arrows above the panel indicate intravenous methylprednisolone pulse
therapy with the dose per pulse course. The dose was tapered to 5 mg/day
within three months and to 2 mg/day at six months after teclistamab.
